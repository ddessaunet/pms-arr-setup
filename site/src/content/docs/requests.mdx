Each open request is checked against Seerr, Radarr or Sonarr, and Prowlarr every time the page loads (kept for a
minute): approved, monitored, out yet, downloading, failed. The ones that need something are on top, under **Needs
attention**; the ones that are fine (downloading, not out yet, waiting for episodes to air) are below, under **On
track**. **Analyse** searches every indexer for it, as Interactive
Search does, and says why each release was refused; the summary is kept for 6 hours. Nothing here changes the apps:
the next step is yours. **Ask AI** (the ✦ **Ask** button) has a model (the one picked above the list) put the same facts into a few
sentences and a next step; it runs only when clicked, one at a time, in the background (a few minutes on this
box's CPU: the page can be left or reloaded), and its answer stays until the facts change.
`jobs/stack-api` answers the page; the verdicts come from `stack/lib/request-doctor.ts`.
